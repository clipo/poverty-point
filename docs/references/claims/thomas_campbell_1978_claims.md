Grey literature; no DOI. Source PDF: `docs/references/pdfs/22-0585 thomas and campbell peripheries.pdf` (305 pp, scanned; OCR text at `…/tc_peripheries.ocr.txt`). LA Division of Archaeology circulating copy.

## Reference correction (resolves comment #4)
- Our reference list previously titled this **"Faunal Analysis of the Poverty Point Sample"** — that is **WRONG**. The OCR title page reads: "THE PERIPHERIES OF POVERTY POINT, by Prentice M. Thomas, Jr. [and] L. Janice Campbell. 1978. New World Research, Report of Investigations No. 12." Reference list now corrected.
- It is an edited site-report volume on peripheral Poverty Point-period sites (Macon Ridge and adjacent floodplains), with a separate **Chapter 11, "Zooarchaeological Remains, by Kathleen M. Byrd" (p. 238)** plus botanical contributions.

## Content used in the manuscript
- The volume's plant-remains (botanical) work underlies the long-cited inference that the peripheral Poverty Point components were occupied in fall-winter (tied to Macon Ridge nut masts), with band dispersal inferred for spring (as characterized by Jackson 1986:Thomas and Campbell 1978:274). This is why the manuscript cites it for a "plant-remains" seasonality reading — defensible, given the volume covered botanical remains. Only the title we assigned was in error.
- This peripheral-site fall-winter / spring-dispersal inference is now revised by type-site macrobotanical evidence (early-summer cultigens; see [[leone_2014_claims]], [[leone_2020_claims]]) in §1.2/§6.7/§7.4.
